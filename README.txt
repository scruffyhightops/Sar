Archive:
- Dedicated CASE ARCHIVE entry from Home.
- Completed cases are saved under a separate archive namespace.
- Active case and archive storage are independent.
- Search by case ID, location, date/time or field unit.
- Archived cases open read-only.
- Timeline and evidence register are retained.
- Viewing an archive never replaces the active case.
